# 🎨 Learning Atom: Exploring Color Theory

## 🎯 Objective
Understand how primary, secondary, and complementary colors interact to create balanced compositions.

---

## 📚 Resource Stack
- **Read:** ["Color Wheel Basics"](https://www.colormatters.com/color-and-design/basic-color-theory) article introducing primary and secondary colors
- **Listen:** Short podcast episode on the emotional impact of different hues
- **Watch:** Video demonstration of mixing paints to build a color wheel
- **Practice:** Create a digital color wheel using free tools like Canva or GIMP
- **Evaluate:** Quiz identifying color harmonies and complementary pairs
- **Collaborate:** Share your color wheel in the community channel and give feedback to a peer

---

## ✅ Completion Criteria
- [ ] Digital color wheel uploaded
- [ ] Quiz completed with 80% or higher
- [ ] Commented on a peer's color wheel
