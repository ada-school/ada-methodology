# 🎨 Learning Atom: Exploring Color Theory

## 🎯 Objective

Explain how primary, secondary, and complementary colors interact to create balanced compositions. *(Bloom: Understand)*

---

## 🔑 Job Competency Link

This atom develops foundational knowledge in **visual design and art fundamentals**, supporting broader competencies in **graphic design, digital illustration, and creative industries**.

---

## 📚 Resource Stack

* **Read:** ["Color Wheel Basics"](https://www.colormatters.com/color-and-design/basic-color-theory) article introducing primary and secondary colors.
* **Listen:** Short podcast episode on the emotional impact of different hues.
* **Watch:** Video demonstration of mixing paints to build a color wheel.
* **Practice:** Create a digital color wheel using free tools like Canva, Figma, or GIMP.
* **Evaluate:** Quiz identifying color harmonies and complementary pairs.
* **Collaborate:** Share your color wheel in the community channel and give feedback to a peer.

---

## 🧪 Practice Activity

Learners design their own **digital color wheel** and use it to create a **simple composition (poster, illustration, or logo)** demonstrating at least one complementary pair.

---

## 📊 Evaluation (Mini-Rubric)

| Criterion       | Excellent (3)                                                          | Adequate (2)                                  | Needs Improvement (1)                |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Accuracy**    | Correct identification of primary, secondary, and complementary colors | Minor mistakes in color placement             | Several mistakes or missing elements |
| **Application** | Demonstrates clear use of color theory in the composition              | Partial use of color theory, somewhat applied | Weak or missing application          |
| **Clarity**     | Clean, professional, well-organized submission                         | Some clarity, could be improved               | Unclear, messy, or hard to interpret |
