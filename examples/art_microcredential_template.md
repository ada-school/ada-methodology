# 🎨 ADA Micro-Credential: Fundamentals of Color Theory in Art

### Applied Digital Apprenticeship (ADA Methodology)

This micro-credential develops foundational **art and design competencies**, focusing on **color theory** as a building block for broader creative skills in graphic design, illustration, and visual communication.

---

## 🎓 Title
**Fundamentals of Color Theory in Art**

---

## ⏳ Estimated Duration
10–15 hours · 2–3 weeks (5h/week)

---

## 🎯 Target Job Competency
Ability to apply **color theory principles** to create balanced, visually effective compositions across artistic and design contexts.

Linked to frameworks:  
- [ESCO – Graphic Designer](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/classification/occupation?uri=http%3A%2F%2Fdata.europa.eu%2Fesco%2Foccupation%2Fd1a6ddad-3f0c-42d5-b68a-c674f085bb21)  
- [O*NET – Graphic Designers (27-1024.00)](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/27-1024.00)  
- [ILO – Creative Industries Skills](https://www.ilo.org/skills/lang--en/index.htm)

---

## 🔑 Prerequisites
- [ ] Basic familiarity with visual arts (painting, drawing, or digital tools)  
- [ ] Access to free design software (Canva, Figma, or GIMP)

---

## 📘 Learning Objectives (Bloom-Aligned)
- **Understand** how primary, secondary, and complementary colors interact.  
- **Apply** color harmonies to create balanced compositions.  
- **Analyze** the emotional and psychological effects of colors.  
- **Create** a digital artwork using intentional color schemes.

---

## 💡 Skills to Be Developed
- Recognize and use primary, secondary, and complementary colors.  
- Build a digital color wheel.  
- Apply color harmonies in artistic projects.  
- Critique peer artworks based on color use.

---

## ⚛ Learning Atoms

### Atom 1: Exploring Color Theory *(Bloom: Understand)*
- **Read:** [Color Wheel Basics](https://www.colormatters.com/color-and-design/basic-color-theory)  
- **Listen:** Podcast on the emotional impact of colors  
- **Watch:** Video demo of mixing paints into a color wheel  
- **Practice:** Create a digital color wheel in Canva/Figma/GIMP  
- **Evaluate:** Quiz on identifying harmonies & complementary pairs  
- **Collaborate:** Share your color wheel and give peer feedback  

👉 See: [Learning Atom: Exploring Color Theory](../examples/learning-atom-art.md)

---

## 🔍 ADA Learning Phases

### 🙉 Phase 1: Self-Guided Introduction  
Learners explore **basic color theory** through readings, videos, and podcasts.

### 🙈 Phase 2: Visual Exploration  
Learners **see color harmonies in action** through case studies, demos, and visual examples.

### 🙊 Phase 3: Applied Practice  
Learners **create their own color wheels** and apply them in small art exercises.

### 🐵 Phase 4: Collaboration & Reflection  
Learners **share and critique peer work** in the community space.

## 🚀 Capstone Project
**Project:** Design a **poster or digital artwork** using at least one complementary color pair.  
**Requirements:**  
- Include a screenshot of your digital color wheel.  
- Explain your choice of colors and their intended emotional impact.  
- Share your poster in the peer community for feedback.

---

## 📊 Assessment & Evaluation

- ✅ Quiz on color harmonies (80%+)  
- ✅ Completed digital color wheel  
- ✅ Poster/artwork submission  
- ✅ Peer review participation  

### Rubric (Capstone Project)
| Criterion | Excellent (5) | Good (3–4) | Needs Improvement (1–2) | Weight |
|-----------|---------------|------------|--------------------------|--------|
| **Relevance (Job Competency Alignment)** | Artwork demonstrates strong understanding of color theory | Mostly correct, some gaps | Weak or missing application | 20% |
| **Application of Skills** | Correct use of color harmonies | Mostly correct, minor mistakes | Limited or incorrect application | 25% |
| **Problem-Solving & Creativity** | Creative, visually appealing solution | Adequate but conventional | Limited originality | 20% |
| **Clarity & Communication** | Clear, professional presentation | Some issues in clarity | Unclear or poorly presented | 15% |
| **Collaboration & Reflection** | Peer review completed with insightful feedback | Peer review done, limited depth | No peer review | 20% |
