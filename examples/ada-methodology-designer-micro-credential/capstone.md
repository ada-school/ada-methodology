# 🚀 Capstone — *Design a Complete ADA Credential*

The capstone is delivered as [Atom 7](atoms/atom-7-capstone-assemble-the-credential.md). This file
is the **grading brief**: what the designer submits, how it maps to KSA, and the showcase format.

---

## 🎯 The brief

> Take a **real organizational skill need** and design a complete ADA micro-credential for it,
> using all three templates — from a Step 0 skill-need intake through KSA breakdown, Bloom-tagged
> objectives, 4–8 atoms, a capstone, a 5-criteria Assessment Rubric, and a badge → skills map — then
> peer-review a classmate's credential and do a 5-minute showcase.

The point is a **coherent, conformant, deliverable** credential someone could actually run — not a
huge one. This is exactly the job: turn a fuzzy "we need people who can do X" into a measurable,
job-matchable learning unit.

---

## 📦 What to submit

- [ ] **The micro-credential doc** — Step 0 intake → competency (SFIA/O\*NET/ESCO) → KSA breakdown
  with 0–4 targets → Bloom + KSA objectives → atoms table → capstone → badge.
- [ ] **4–8 learning atoms** — at least one **Knowledge**, one **Skill**, and one **Ability** atom,
  each with topology-correct modalities and a matching evaluation.
- [ ] **A 5-criteria Assessment Rubric** for the capstone (+ at least one mini-rubric).
- [ ] **A badge → skills-map fragment** showing which KSA levels completion certifies.
- [ ] **The Design Conformance Checklist** (from the template) passed.
- [ ] **A peer review you gave** — applied to a classmate's credential.

---

## 🎤 Showcase structure (≈ 5 minutes)

1. **The skill need** (45s) — whose job, which task, why now.
2. **KSA breakdown** (60s) — the components and target levels.
3. **One atom** (90s) — your proudest atom and why its modalities fit its KSA type.
4. **Badge → job gap** (60s) — what the skills map writes and the requirement it closes.
5. **Human-in-the-loop** (15s) — what a mentor/employer must validate before it counts.

## ✅ Grading

Atom-level checks (`knowledge-mini`, `skill`) gate entry to the capstone, which is then scored on the
standard 5-criteria **Assessment Rubric** below (also in [`rubrics.md`](rubrics.md)).

## 📋 Assessment Rubric

Five criteria across four proficiency bands, weighted to **100 points**.

| Criteria | Excellent (100–90%) | Competent (89–80%) | Developing (79–70%) | Initial (69% or less) | Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Relevance (competency alignment)** | Anchored to a real skill need and a named framework; alignment is tight from intake to badge. | Relevant and framework-linked; minor gaps. | Loosely relevant; framework link weak or generic. | Off-target or unanchored to any real need. | **20 pts** |
| **Application of skills** | KSA typing, Bloom objectives, modality fit, and rubric design are all correct and consistent. | Mostly correct with minor errors. | Several gaps (mistyped KSA, fuzzy verbs, modality mismatch, vague bands). | Minimal or incorrect application of the methodology. | **25 pts** |
| **Problem-solving & creativity** | Atoms are well-sequenced and inventive; the capstone genuinely simulates the real job task. | Sound, conventional design that works. | Uneven sequence; thin or generic capstone. | Incoherent sequence or missing capstone. | **20 pts** |
| **Clarity & communication** | Docs are clear, on-template, and professional; showcase is crisp and persuasive. | Generally clear and on-template. | Uneven clarity or off-template in places. | Unclear, incomplete, or not on-template. | **15 pts** |
| **Collaboration & reflection** | Insightful peer review given; honest reflection; human-in-the-loop points explicitly flagged. | Adequate peer review and reflection. | Minimal review / reflection. | Missing peer review or reflection. | **20 pts** |
| **TOTAL** | | | | | **100 pts** |

> **Pass for the badge:** atom-level `skill` checks ≥ 2 each AND the Assessment Rubric weighted score
> ≥ 70% (at least *Developing* on every criterion), with the peer review + showcase completed —
> **verified by a mentor** (human-in-the-loop). See [`skills-map.md`](skills-map.md) for what the
> badge unlocks.
