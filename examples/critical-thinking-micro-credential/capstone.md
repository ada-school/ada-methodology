# 🚀 Capstone — Reason Through a Real Issue

The capstone integrates every component of the **Critical Thinking** micro-credential into one piece of
evidence. Full task and rubric live in
[`atoms/atom-6-capstone-reason-through-a-real-issue.md`](atoms/atom-6-capstone-reason-through-a-real-issue.md);
this file is the at-a-glance brief and the KSA mapping.

---

## 🎯 The brief

> **Take a real, contested issue, evaluate the evidence on all sides, reach a reasoned and calibrated
> conclusion, and defend it under peer scrutiny.**

Deliverable: a **Reasoned Position Brief** (~800–1,200 words or short deck) that:

1. **Frames** a genuinely contested issue (and why it's contested).
2. **Steelmans all sides** — strongest argument for each, represented fairly.
3. **Evaluates the evidence** — quality, fallacies/biases, lateral verification with cited sources.
4. **Builds a reasoned position** — claim → reasons → evidence → strongest counterargument + honest
   response → **qualified** conclusion.
5. **States what would change your mind** + your confidence level.
6. **Reflects** on whether and how your view shifted.

Plus a **showcase/defense** and at least one **peer review**.

| --- | --------- | --------------------------------- |
| 🧠 K | The anatomy of reasoning | Correctly maps claims/premises/assumptions across sides |
| 🧠 K | Fallacies & biases | Names fallacies/biases in the evidence and arguments |
| 🛠️ S | Evaluate arguments & evidence | The evidence-evaluation section (quality, relevance, verification) |
| 🛠️ S | Question & verify | Lateral verification of key evidence; Socratic framing; steelmans |
| 🛠️ S | Build & defend an argument | The reasoned-position section + live defense |
| 🌱 A | Intellectual humility | Calibration; "what would change my mind"; conceding/ updating in defense |
| 🌱 A | Skeptical curiosity | Questioning all sides incl. own; sharp peer review |
